## 8. Financial Policies, Installment Plans, & Payment Modes

* **Accepted Payment Modes:**
  * Cash (Philippine Peso / USD at prevailing BSP rate)
  * Major Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB)
  * Digital Wallets (GCash, Maya, QR Ph)
  * Bank Transfer (BDO, BPI, UnionBank InstaPay/PESONet)
* **0% Interest Installment Financing:**
  * Available on major credit cards (BDO, BPI, Metrobank, HSBC) for procedures ₱20,000 and above (3, 6, 12, or 24 months terms).
* **Appointment Cancellation & No-Show Policy:**
  * Rescheduling requires a minimum of **24 hours prior notice**. 
  * Unattended appointments past 1 hour of the scheduled time slot are automatically logged as `no_show` in the central clinical registry.
