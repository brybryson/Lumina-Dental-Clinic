## 1. Protocol A: Oral Surgery, Tooth Extractions & Surgical Wisdom Tooth Removal (Odontectomy)

### Immediate Care (First 2 to 4 Hours)
* **Gauze Pressure:** Keep firm, steady biting pressure on the sterile cotton gauze pad placed over the surgical site for **45 to 60 minutes**. If oozing continues, replace with a fresh folded sterile gauze pad and bite down firmly for another 45 minutes.
* **Moist Tea Bag Technique (Alternative Hemostasis):** If light bleeding persists after 2 hours, place a moistened black tea bag (e.g. Lipton / Earl Grey) over the extraction site and bite firmly for 30 minutes. Tannic acid in black tea naturally constricts blood vessels and accelerates clot formation.
* **Do NOT Spit or Rinse:** Swallow saliva naturally. Spitting generates negative intraoral suction which will dislodge the fragile blood clot, resulting in painful **Dry Socket (Alveolar Osteitis)**.

### Blood Clot Protection & Strict Prohibitions (First 72 Hours)
* **NO Drinking Through Straws:** The vacuum created inside the mouth will suction the clot directly out of the extraction socket.
* **NO Smoking, Vaping, or Tobacco:** Nicotine impairs microvascular blood flow and drastically delays healing; the physical inhalation pressure dislodges the clot.
* **NO Vigorous Rinsing or Swishing:** Avoid mouthwashes containing alcohol for at least 7 days.
* **NO Strenuous Physical Exercise or Heavy Lifting:** Increased blood pressure induces renewed bleeding at the surgical site.

### Swelling & Pain Management
* **Cold Therapy (Hours 1 to 24):** Apply an ice pack (or frozen gel compress wrapped in a clean cloth) to the external cheek area adjacent to the surgical site. Alternate **20 minutes ON, 20 minutes OFF** while awake. Maximum swelling typically peaks between 48 to 72 hours post-surgery.
* **Heat Therapy (After 48 Hours):** Switch to moist warm compresses applied to the outer jaw to stimulate blood circulation and relieve residual muscle stiffness (trismus).
* **Medication Schedule:**
  * Take prescribed analgesics (e.g. Ibuprofen 400–600mg or Mefenamic Acid 500mg) **before the local anesthesia wears off**.
  * If antibiotics (e.g. Amoxicillin / Clindamycin) were prescribed, complete the entire 7-day course without skipping doses, even if discomfort resolves early.

### Diet & Nutrition Guidelines
* **First 24 to 48 Hours:** Strictly cold or room-temperature soft foods: Greek yogurt, cold broths, smoothies (eaten with a spoon, NOT a straw), mashed potatoes, chilled oatmeal, scrambled eggs, and ice cream.
* **Avoid:** Hot soups, spicy foods, acidic citrus juices, hard nuts, chips, popcorn, or foods with small seeds that can lodge in the extraction socket.

### Oral Hygiene from Day 2 Onwards
* **Gentle Warm Saline Rinses:** Beginning 24 hours after surgery, gently bathe the mouth with warm salt water (1/2 teaspoon of salt in 1 cup of warm water) **after every meal and before bedtime** (4–5 times daily). Tilt head side-to-side and let the water passively spill out into the sink.
* **Brushing:** Brush adjacent teeth gently with an ultra-soft toothbrush, taking extreme care to avoid touching the surgical wound or sutures.

## 2. Protocol B: In-Clinic Laser Teeth Whitening Post-Care ("The White Diet")

Following professional in-chair laser whitening (e.g. Philips Zoom / Lumina Dual-Light), the tooth enamel's microscopic protective pellicle layer is temporarily demineralized and dehydrated for **48 hours**, making teeth highly susceptible to absorbing external pigments.

### The Strict 48-Hour "White Diet"
| Food / Beverage Category | Approved Safe Items (EAT / DRINK) | Forbidden Staining Agents (AVOID STRICTLY) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beverages** | Plain water, skim milk, sparkling water, clear coconut water | Coffee, black/green tea, red wine, cola, dark beer, colored fruit juices |
| **Proteins** | Skinless chicken breast, white fish (cod, tilapia), egg whites, plain tofu | Beef, dark poultry, marinated meats with soy sauce or dark spices |
| **Carbohydrates** | White rice, plain pasta with white/cream sauce, white bread, mashed potatoes | Pasta with red marinara sauce, curries, soy-seasoned noodles |
| **Dairy** | White cheese, plain yogurt, cottage cheese, butter | Cheddar, colored processed cheese, fruit-infused yogurts |
| **Fruits & Veggies** | Bananas, peeled apples, cauliflower, white onions | Berries (blueberries, strawberries), beetroot, tomatoes, spinach |
| **Condiments** | Salt, white pepper, mayonnaise, sour cream | Soy sauce, ketchup, mustard, balsamic vinegar, chili sauce, curry paste |

### Managing Post-Whitening Tooth Sensitivity
* Mild to moderate transient "zingers" (fleeting nerve sensitivity) are normal for the first 24 to 48 hours.
* Apply Lumina Relief ACP Gel or brush with Potassium Nitrate desensitizing toothpaste (Sensodyne / Colgate Pro-Relief).
* Avoid excessively hot or icy-cold liquids during the initial 48-hour recovery window.

## 3. Protocol C: Dental Prophylaxis & Deep Periodontal Scaling (SRP)

* **Gingival Tenderness:** Mild gum soreness and slight bleeding during brushing are normal for 24 to 48 hours as inflamed periodontal tissues heal.
* **Saline Rinses:** Rinse with warm salt water 2–3 times daily to soothe irritated gum margins.
* **Flossing & Brushing:** Continue daily flossing and gentle brushing. Do not stop cleaning because of light bleeding; clean surfaces are essential for gum reattachment.
* **Sensitivity to Temperature:** Roots cleaned of tartar deposits may react to cold water. Use a soft-bristled brush and sensitivity toothpaste.

---

## 4. Protocol D: Endodontic Therapy (Root Canal) & Temporary Restorations

* **Temporary Filling Caution:** The temporary filling placed between endodontic appointments requires 1 hour to fully set. Avoid chewing food on that side of your mouth until the final permanent crown/restoration is cemented.
* **Normal Post-Treatment Sensation:** Tenderness when biting down or chewing is common for 3 to 5 days due to periapical ligament inflammation. Over-the-counter anti-inflammatory medication (Ibuprofen / Paracetamol) effectively manages this.
* **Crown Protection:** Teeth treated with root canal therapy become brittle over time. Schedule your permanent crown within 2 to 4 weeks to prevent catastrophic tooth fracture.

## 5. Protocol E: Red-Flag Clinical Emergencies — When to Contact the Clinic Immediately

Patients experiencing any of the following symptoms should immediately contact the **Lumina 24/7 Clinical Hotline** at **(02) 8888-LUMI** or **+63 917 123 4567**:

1. **Persistent Heavy Bleeding:** Active, continuous dark-red pooling of blood that fills the mouth within minutes and does not subside after 2 rounds of firm gauze/tea bag pressure.
2. **Elevated Fever:** Body temperature exceeding **38.5°C (101.3°F)** accompanied by chills or malaise.
3. **Severe Uncontrolled Pain:** Throbbing, radiating pain that persists unabated despite taking prescribed pain medications at maximum allowable dosages.
4. **Progressive Facial Swelling:** Rapid swelling spreading into the submandibular neck space, under the eye, or causing difficulty swallowing or breathing (**requires immediate emergency room evaluation**).
5. **Loss of Temporary Filling / Dislodged Crown:** Prevents contamination of the root canals or sensitivity.
